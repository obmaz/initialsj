Audio credits and licenses

impact.wav
  Godot Engine contributors, Truck Town demonstration project
  https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects/tree/master/3d/truck_town/vehicles
  Source: impact_1.wav. MIT license; full text in GODOT-LICENSE.txt.
  Changes: filtering, mono resampling, trimming and fade-out.

countdown.wav, launch.wav, reward.wav, lap.wav
  Original short synthesized UI cues baked by tools/bake_cues.py.
  These files belong to this project; no third-party recordings are included.

vehicles/<id>-engine.ogg, -boost.ogg, -boost-start.wav, -drift.ogg, web-loops.json
  Original sounds synthesized by tools/bake_vehicle_audio.py: one engine, booster,
  ignition hit and drift sound per vehicle. No third-party recordings are included, and
  web-loops.json only repackages the baked loops. The shared engine and tyre recordings
  (OpenGameArt, CC BY 3.0, by qubodup and audible-edge) that earlier builds used were
  replaced by these and removed; they remain credited in the Git history.
  Adding a vehicle adds its four files here: see docs/VEHICLE_AUDIO.md.
