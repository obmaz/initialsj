# Regional 3D foreground review

The roadside foreground now uses solid, opaque meshes. The Tour has 18 regional prop models: palms, broadleaf and plane trees, ginkgo, conifers, Japanese pines, cherry trees, bamboo, snow-covered conifers, cacti, basalt and sandstone formations, charred trees, obelisks, ruined columns, small shops, ticket booths and container stacks. Each destination selects an appropriate mix instead of placing one generated cutout repeatedly.

Fuji has 104 placed 3D trees across conifers, spreading Japanese pines and cherry trees. Its rare landmarks are four additional mesh types: torii gates, pagodas, tea houses and low red bridges. Roadside placements use each model's full horizontal bounding radius and check the nearest road centre before accepting the object. Expanded dirt shoulders and guardrail setbacks remain clear.

City, Seoul, neon and ruined-city architecture retains six facade archetypes with low-rise blocks, glass slabs, setback towers, cylindrical towers, mid-rise roofs and mixed-use blocks. Seoul varies apartment balconies and pale concrete; the neon district uses darker facades and lit windows. Foreground foundations now account for the whole building footprint and the local rail setback. The urban skyline cutouts have also been replaced with low-detail 3D blocks, cylinders and stepped towers. Generated distant mountain/ridge images and the Fuji sky remain horizon backdrops.

The meshes are cached once per model, share one opaque vertex-colour material and use sRGB vertex colours. Small props have no shadow pass or per-object lights. Instances are grouped into 180m spatial cells with at most 12 objects per MultiMesh, and use a 310–400m visibility range. The city skyline uses four lightweight groups. This avoids a single giant foreground batch submitting the whole route and removes foreground alpha blending/billboard rotations.

`tests/scenery_3d_checks.gd` passes all 15 regional mixes, non-flat mesh bounds, per-model triangle budgets, instance caps, six representative actual route clearances, city silhouette variety, 3D urban skylines, the Fuji forest and solid landmarks. Native captures in `regional-3d-palm-coast.png`, `regional-3d-city.png` and `regional-3d-fuji.png` were inspected after rendering. `scenery-3d-render-metrics.json` records draw submissions for those fixed views; it is not an Android/browser FPS measurement or a before/after benchmark.
